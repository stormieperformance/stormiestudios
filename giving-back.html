Giving Back

Free websites for animal & wildlife welfare organizations.

I've had animals in my life for as long as I can remember. I've worked as a vet nurse assistant, managed a dog day care, and today I volunteer as an animal caretaker at a non-profit farm, caring for their farm animals on weekends. Giving back to the people who dedicate their lives to protecting animals feels like the right place to start.

As Stormie Studios grows, I'm offering 2-3 custom websites completely free each year to registered non-profit organizations working in animal rescue, wildlife conservation, or animal welfare.

Every website is designed and built by me from scratch, so I can only take on a small number of projects. If your organization could benefit from a new website, I'd love to hear from you.
