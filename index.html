Faq

Common questions.

How long does a project take?+

Most projects launch within a few weeks of our first call, depending on scope and how quickly content comes together.

Do you provide hosting?+

Yes, always. Hosting, security, backups and updates are handled by me for one flat monthly fee, that's how every site stays fast, secure and online.

Can you use my existing domain?+

Yes, I connect your existing domain and set up HTTPS as part of the build.

Can I update the website myself?+

Yes — hero video, photos, pricing, staff or coach profiles, class listings, campaign banners, all of it. Nearly every image and text block across the site is yours to update directly, no developer needed, and nothing breaks even if you leave a field blank.

Do you work outside Stockholm?+

Absolutely. Most of my clients are here in Stockholm today, but I work just as easily with clients anywhere else, over video calls and email.

Why not just use an AI website builder?+

An AI builder can generate a page in minutes, but it doesn't know how your gym actually runs. I build the schedule, membership paths and content system around your real workflow, not a generic template, and I'm a person you can reach directly when something needs to change or something breaks.

Who owns the website and the code?+

Your domain, brand, copy, images, videos and business content are always yours. Stormie Studios retains and maintains the underlying source code, development system and production setup. You're buying a custom-built, professionally managed website and the right to use it for your business, not taking on the technical responsibility of maintaining the software behind it. You remain in control of your content through the CMS, while I remain responsible for the underlying build.

Can our IT team manage the domain and review the setup?+

Absolutely. Your organization can retain control of its domain, DNS and relevant third-party accounts. I'm happy to provide the technical information needed for IT or security review, including hosting, integrations, data flows and deployment architecture. The production source repository and build system remain managed by Stormie Studios, while your IT team retains control of the infrastructure and accounts that belong to your organization.
