About

Built from the gym floor.

I'm Storm Kolmodin. Licensed personal trainer, agile project manager, and developer. I build websites for gyms, clubs, and coaches. Not because I found a niche, but because it's the world I come from.

Stormie Studios is a small studio with a simple promise: you'll work directly with the person building your site. No middlemen. No handovers. No surprises.

— Storm

Why it works
i. Licensed Personal Trainer

Certified since 2018 and further educated at the Swedish School of Sport and Health Sciences (GIH). I know what makes someone dare to book their first session.

ii. Agile Project Manager & Developer

Trained at Futuregames and Stockholm University. Deadlines kept. No surprises. You get a working site, not a project that never gets finished.

iii. Former Gym Manager & Fitness Instructor

Working in fitness since 2017. I have been on the floor, managed memberships, planned schedules and run operations. I understand the industry from the inside.

iv. Built From the Industry

Every project draws on real time in the industry, not just building for it. After years in the fitness industry as a personal trainer, fitness instructor, gym manager and coach, I understand how the business works, for both members and staff.
